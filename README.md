# Figsaw

Three smaller, dimly lit Figpals hang above large glass-sided green canisters in a staggered bay on the left, clear of Figsaw’s dialogue: a party-hat pudding, an orange-beanie sushi, and a propeller-hat pufferfish. Their 3D models retain the illustrated colors, hats and black outlines, with anxious brows, frowns and a frightened open mouth. Each is supported by interlocking steel chain slings, a spreader bar, and an overhead motorized winch with a sprocket, return chain, crank and inspection light. Click a winch or its canister control, or press 1, 2 or 3 while the scene has focus, to lower or raise that Figpal. The chain pays out and the drum rotates as the character descends into the liquid. Winch housings, motor fins, sprockets and oval chain links use correlated rust, pitting, roughness and metalness textures, with greasy bearing seals, hex bolts and worn service plates. The foggy glass canisters have mineral runs, algae waterlines, condensation and sediment. Murky green liquid has a reflective, moving surface, slowly shifting currents, small rising bubbles, sparse foam and fading ripples. Each vat holds a different collection of stained ivory remains: a skull and femur in the first, a broken rib cage, spine and loose rib in the second, and a smaller tilted skull, forearm bone and detached jaw in the third, slowly drifting, bobbing and turning beneath the surface. Skulls have exaggerated domed heads, oversized mismatched hollow sockets, crooked buck teeth and a lopsided jaw for a comical expression. Lowering a Figpal reveals its skeleton progressively at the waterline, with the chain sling still holding it; the pufferfish has a fish spine, curved ribs and bony tail. The converted parts remain bone when the hoist is raised, until the scene is reloaded. Their paths stay inside the liquid and freeze in reduced-motion mode. Glass, water and industrial metal reflect the furnished room from the same one-time environment capture used by the monitors. Reduced-motion mode freezes ambient animation and moves the hoists immediately. Furniture is rearranged to clear the left bay. The default camera and Figsaw’s stopping position are unchanged.

All models and wear textures are generated locally, with no remote asset dependencies. A small Copy view button in the top-right exports the current camera as a paste-ready prompt. The visual references are [dasy444’s abandoned office](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/office-is-old-abandoned-free-3977a108b2304b85a698f3dda1777015) and the [Neo apartment recreation](https://www.reddit.com/r/ClaudeAI/comments/1x6x6x7/3d_recreation_of_neo_apartment_from_the_matrix/); their model and texture files are not bundled. The chairs use [wearexyz’s Aeron model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/herman-miller-aeron-chair-low-poly-transparency-b28343475d154cb8ba40066b83e28f7f) as a shape reference; the chair geometry and woven textures are original procedural assets. A compact worn black digital timer sits farther to the right of the laptop on the floor, scaled to 72% of its original size and turned 16 degrees clockwise from the saved camera, with bright red seven-segment digits. It waits at 05:00 until Figsaw finishes his arrival line, then automatically opens the laptop and counts down using an absolute monotonic deadline so slow frames and background tabs do not lengthen the five minutes. It stops at 00:00. Pressing R resets the timer and replays the entrance and dialogue before starting again. Reduced-motion mode preserves the same reading pause and countdown.

Use WASD to move relative to the viewing direction, E to raise the camera, and Q to lower it. Drag or use arrow keys to look around freely; scroll to zoom. The default camera position is `[1.85221, 0.821615, 3.1149]`, looking at `[1.293346, 0.717709, 2.292176]` with a 62-degree vertical field of view. Movement stays inside the room and clears on focus loss. Press R to restore the default position, direction and zoom, and replay the entrance. Hold Space to open the mouth or tap Figsaw for a short mouth animation.

Copy view exports the rendered camera position, look-at point, and vertical field of view as JSON prefaced with a request to set that view as the default. The copied values include the current zoom. Paste the text into the chat to request a new default. If clipboard access is unavailable, a small selectable text panel provides the same prompt. The saved position, direction, and vertical field of view apply at every viewport size.

Serve `dist` with any static HTTP server:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 5173 --directory dist --bind 127.0.0.1
```

Three.js 0.169.0 is bundled under `dist/vendor` with its MIT license. Grenze Gotisch is bundled under `dist/fonts` with its SIL Open Font License. No build step, external fonts, or API key is required.

Lowering an individual Figpal with its winch, canister control, or 1 / 2 / 3 also triggers its own panicked speech bubble. Each bubble has an independent lifetime; raising a Figpal clears its plea, and lowering it again restarts it. The timer finale still triggers all three together.
